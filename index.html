Sambahsa for English Speakers

This learning course is inspired by Margarita Madrigal and Language Transfer.

With very little grammatical explanation, you will begin to internalize the language. Don't try to memorize anything! Just take your time and really think through how to translate the phrases. And mistakes are ok! Each lesson will build upon what you learned before it. Think of this course like a puzzle. You are building neural pathways :)

Lesson 1

The verb stem for want is eiskw.

To say "I want", you just add an "o" on the end.

I want.

Eiskwo. (pronounced like "ace-kwo")

To say "you want", you just add an "s" on the end.

You want.

Eiskws. (pronounced like "ace-kus")

The verb stem for love is lieubh.

Just like before, to say "I love", you add an "o" on the end.

I love.

Lieubho. (pronounced like "lyoob-ho")

You love.

Lieubhs. (pronounced like "lyoobs")

To negate a verb, you place ne (pronounced like "neh") before it.

I don't want.

Ne eiskwo.

You don't love.

Ne lieubhs.

The word for him is iom (pronounced like "yom")

I love him.

Lieubho iom.

You don't want him.

Ne eiskws iom.

In this lesson, you have learned:

  • eiskw-
  • lieubh-
  • -o
  • -s
  • ne
  • iom

Lesson 2

When you want to say he, she, or it does something, you add a -t to the verb stem.

He/She/It wants.

Eiskwt.

He/She/It loves.

Lieubht.

To specify if the doer is a man, woman, or it, you can use the pronoun before.

The word for he is is (pronounced like "ease" but with a true s, not a z sound.)

The word for she is ia (pronounced like "ya")

He loves him.

Is lieubht iom.

She wants him.

Ia lieubht iom.

The word for her is iam (pronounced like "yahm")

You love her.

Lieubhs iam.

He loves her.

Is lieubht iam.

The word for me is me (pronounced like "meh")

She wants me.

Ia eiskwt me.

You love me.

Lieubhs me.

In this lesson, you have learned:

  • -t
  • is
  • ia
  • iam
  • me

Lesson 3

The word for you is te (pronounced like "teh")

I love you.

Lieubho te.

She wants you.

Ia eiskwt te.

You love him.

Lieubhs iom.

In this lesson, you have learned:

  • te
